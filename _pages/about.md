I am a PhD student in Arabic Linguistics at Georgetown University. My research lies at the interface of linguistic theory and computational modeling, with a particular focus on Arabic morphosyntax and natural language processing.

I am interested in how grammatical generalizations can be translated into computational representations while preserving the structural and dialectal complexity of Arabic. My current research focuses on:

* morpho-syntactic agreement in Modern Standard Arabic and Arabic dialects;
* computational approaches to grammatical variation;
* causative–anticausative alternations and argument structure; and
* linguistically informed evaluation and resource development for Arabic NLP.

I combine formal linguistic analysis with corpus-based research, linguistic annotation, computational modeling, and the evaluation of language technologies.
